Governance & security

Protect the work.
Keep what matters.

Protect your people, information, and systems. Give AI clear boundaries, and make sure someone owns what happens next.

What this means for your business ↓

Why it matters

AI goes where your data goes.

Every AI tool your people use can reach something. Know what it can reach, who approved it and who responds when something goes wrong, and AI becomes safe to use across the business.

Guardrails also keep what AI helps create. The prompts, drafts and know-how your people build on company time belong to the business. Without governance, they sit in personal accounts and unapproved tools, and they leave when people do.

Connected data

When data comes together, the rules have to come with it.

Companies are moving data out of separate systems and into shared data platforms so AI can work across all of it. That is what makes AI useful. It also means one question can now reach finance, HR and customer records at once.

Each system used to guard its own data. Once the data moves, those permissions don't follow on their own. Classification, access and audit have to be built into the platform and every AI tool that reads from it.

What good looks like

Signs the guardrails hold.

You know what AI can reach.

An inventory of the AI tools in use, approved or not, and the data each one can touch.

Permissions travel with the data.

Access is set by role and holds as data moves between systems, platforms and AI tools. Consequential actions need a person's approval.

What people create stays with the business.

Approved places to work with AI, so prompts, drafts and new knowledge are kept, findable and reviewed.

Someone owns the response.

Alerts reach a team that can revoke access, isolate a system or pause an agent. Recovery is tested before it's needed.
