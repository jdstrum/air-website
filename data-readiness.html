WHAT MAKES THE PICTURE USEFUL

Connected is the beginning.
Trusted is the standard.

The data is fit for the work.

Know what the information contains and whether it is complete, consistent, and accurate enough to use. Reconcile different names and definitions, with the people who understand the work helping to define what it means.

Access follows permission.

People and AI should reach the information they are authorized to use. Connecting sources must preserve those boundaries.

The result has a source.

Know where information came from, whether it is current enough, how it changed, and what may be missing or conflicting. Give people a traceable basis for the next step.

Useful data and knowledge can be reused.

Some preparation serves one need. When information supports recurring work, keep it refreshed and maintain the connection instead of rebuilding it. With the right permissions, decisions and insights can inform what happens next.
