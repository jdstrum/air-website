Data readiness

AI is only as useful as the business it can see.

The more of your business AI can see, the better its answers, and the more work it can take on.

See the build order ↓

Why it matters

Your data is scattered. AI needs one picture.

Customer records sit in the CRM, transactions in the ERP, and knowledge in documents, calls and inboxes. Each system was bought to do one job, and few of them talk to each other. AI can only work with what it can reach.

That's why companies are moving their data onto shared data platforms, where information from every system is connected, cleaned and kept current. Data readiness decides whether AI gives answers your people trust.

The build order

Build it in order.

AI sits on top. What’s underneath has to be built first.

What good looks like

Signs your data is ready.

Systems feed one platform.

Data moves automatically from the systems that create it, instead of through exports and spreadsheets.

The data is fit for the work.

Complete, consistent and defined the same way across teams, with the people who know the work deciding what it means.

Every answer has a source.

People can see where information came from and how current it is before they act on it.

Knowledge is findable.

Documents, calls and decisions are searchable, so teams stop rebuilding what the business already knows.
