Privacy notice · updated September 2026

Your information,
plainly handled.

What we collect when you use this site, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

AI Resulting is a trade name of Awe72 LLC, an Arizona limited liability company (“AIR,” “we,” “us”).

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your name, work email, company, and anything you write or upload when you answer the six questions, request a resource, or book a conversation.
  • Your answers. What you choose in the six-question tool, any notes you add, and the company website you give us. We use them to write your results and, if you ask for a conversation, to prepare for it.
  • Basic site data. Pages visited, referring site, device and browser type, collected through standard analytics and server logs.

How we use it

  • To send what you asked for and to respond to you.
  • To prepare for and follow up on a conversation you requested.
  • To understand which pages and resources are useful, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your information. Requesting a resource does not subscribe you to marketing email.

When it is shared

AIR is compensated by technology suppliers (see How we are paid). We do not pass your details or documents to any supplier unless you ask us to involve them.

We use service providers to run the site and our follow-up: website hosting, analytics, email delivery, scheduling, a customer relationship platform, and an AI service that helps write your results. When you complete the six questions, your answers, notes and company website are sent to that AI service to prepare your results. It does not use them to train its models. They process information on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and conversation records while we have an active or prospective relationship with you, and delete or anonymize them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; we will honor them.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies for analytics, to keep the site working, and to remember which advisor shared a link with you. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending information you are not permitted to share, such as regulated personal data about your customers or employees.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@airesulting.com. If we change this notice in a meaningful way, we will update the date above.
