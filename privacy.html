Privacy

AI Resulting (“AIR,” “we,” “us”) collects limited personal information to respond to your requests, prepare for conversations you ask for, send the resources you request, and run this site. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your name, work email, company, company size, and anything you write when you answer the ten questions, request a resource, or ask to talk to a Resultant.
  • Your answers. What you choose in the six-question tool, any notes you add, and the company website you give us. We use them to write your results and, if you ask for a conversation, to prepare for it.
  • Basic site data. Pages visited, referring site, and device and browser type, through cookie-free analytics. Our hosting provider also keeps routine technical logs, such as IP address, browser, requested page and time, for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To send what you asked for and to respond to you.
  • To prepare for and follow up on a conversation you requested.
  • To understand which pages and resources are useful, so we can improve them.
  • To send occasional insights and updates from AI Resulting. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep the site secure, keep business records, and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

  • Resultants. The advisors who work with AIR handle your request. If you arrived through a link an advisor shared with you, that advisor receives your request and results.
  • Service providers. Companies that run the site and our follow-up on our behalf: hosting, analytics, email delivery, scheduling, a customer relationship platform, and an AI service that helps write your results. When you complete the ten questions, your answers, notes and company website go to that AI service to prepare your results; it does not use them to train its models. These providers may not use your information for their own purposes.
  • Technology suppliers. AIR is paid by suppliers (see How we are paid). We do not pass your details or documents to a supplier unless you ask us to involve them.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect rights, security or property, or to prevent fraud. Information may also transfer if AIR is reorganized, merged or sold.

Cookies and tracking

We don’t use advertising pixels, tracking cookies or behavioral advertising. Our analytics don’t use cookies. If you arrive through a link an advisor shared with you, the site sets one cookie so we know who shared it.

How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep contact and conversation records while we have an active or prospective relationship with you, and as long as we reasonably need them for business and legal records. Then we delete or anonymize them.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure. Please don’t send information you aren’t permitted to share, such as regulated personal data about your customers or employees.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, and we will honor them. We may need to verify your identity first. We won’t treat you differently for making a request. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link.

Where we’re based

AIR is based in the United States, and information is processed and stored here. If you’re visiting from elsewhere, privacy laws here may differ from yours.
