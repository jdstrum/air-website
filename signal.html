Where the conversation is going.

These are not broad forecasts. They are the questions creating projects, budgets, and supplier decisions now.

Data foundation

Who organizes all of our data for AI?

The answer spans structured records, documents, permissions, integration, and the question of which source deserves trust. The data platform is only one part of the job.

Security & control

How do we protect what employees are doing with AI?

Organizations need visibility into which tools are being used, what information is leaving the company, and how access and policy follow the employee.

Compute & cost

Should we buy GPU capacity or pay for model usage?

The answer turns on workload, data sensitivity, performance, control, utilization, and token economics. Many businesses will use a blend.

Customer & employee experience

Where will AI improve an actual interaction?

Customer service, contact center, collaboration, and internal support often meet at the same underlying issue: whether context and knowledge reach the next person in time.
