Experience layer

The next conversation
should start ahead.

Customers should not have to repeat their story. Your people should not have to recreate what the business already knows. Keep useful context moving between both.

What this means for your business ↓

Why it matters

This is where people feel AI.

Customers feel it on a call or in a chat. Staff feel it in meetings, messages and the answer they need mid-conversation. The experience layer is where the rest of the foundation shows up.

Good technology gives people more time to listen, solve and care. Sometimes the answer is AI. Sometimes it's a better connection, a simpler process or clearer ownership.

The experience of work

Make the working day
work better.

Meetings, messages, documents, and internal requests should carry useful context into the next step, without making people search, repeat, or chase.

What good looks like

Signs the experience is working.

Customers never repeat their story.

Context carries from chat to phone to email and into the customer record.

The person helping has a head start.

Approved knowledge, records and next steps appear in the live conversation.

Automation handles what it can finish.

Assistants take routine requests, say they're AI and hand over with context when a person is needed.

Both sides measure it.

Resolution, effort and workload are tracked alongside cost, with customers and staff asked directly.
