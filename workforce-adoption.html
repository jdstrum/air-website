Workforce & adoption

Good practice should
travel.

AI becomes useful when people see its place in their work. Help a useful individual practice become a shared capability the organization can build on.

What this means for your business ↓

In practice

Useful practice should spread.

Licenses alone do not tell you whether adoption is working. Look at what people can do, what leaders support, and what survives beyond one person’s screen.

Learning belongs in the role.

Use relevant tasks and ongoing practice. People need time, approved tools, and somewhere to bring questions as the work changes.

Leadership makes it concrete.

Leaders make time for learning and connect the work to a real outcome. Listen to concerns about quality, autonomy, workload, and changing roles; those concerns can improve the plan.

Knowledge should outlast the individual.

Make approved workflows and lessons easy to find and maintain. Keep useful capability available when people change roles.
