The ownership test

A possibility without an owner remains just an idea.

The question is not whether one person does all of this. It is whether the business has made the responsibility visible. The roles can sit in different seats, but all three must be present.

The sponsor

Own the outcome.

Name the business result, give the work authority, and keep it tied to a priority that matters.

The operating owner

Own the change in the work.

Carry the workflow, the people using it, and the measure of whether it made the work better.

The technology steward

Own what has to hold.

Make the data, systems, safeguards, and architecture capable of supporting the work in the real world.

Bring one live situation

Bring the work that is holding the business back.

You do not need a finished use case or a platform decision. Bring the work that is taking too long, breaking down, or asking too much of the people doing it.
