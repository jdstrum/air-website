Strategic direction

The opportunity is limitless.
Choose what matters.

AI can change how the business sees, decides, and operates. The work is choosing where it should, and in what order.

What this means for your business ↓

Why it matters

Direction decides what the rest is for.

Everything underneath AI gets sized to what you want it to do. The data, network, security and training look different for a company speeding up quotes than for one closing the books faster.

Without it, money goes to tools nobody owns and pilots nobody finishes. With it, every purchase has a reason, and part of the answer is often already paid for.

In practice

A possibility needs owners.

You don’t need a finished use case. You need three people, each with a clear piece of the work.

The business sponsor.

Decides what’s worth doing, gives the work authority and keeps it tied to a priority.

The operating owner.

Knows the workflow and the people in it, and turns an idea into how the work actually gets done.

The technology steward.

Makes sure the data, systems and safeguards underneath can carry it.

What good looks like

Signs the direction is set.

The first move is small enough to finish.

One workflow and one team, with a measure agreed before anything is bought.

The foundation is checked before the tool is chosen.

Data, network and security gaps are known before anyone signs a contract.

Every purchase maps to a step.

Nothing gets bought or renewed that isn’t on the plan.

Something gets stopped.

A regular review of what’s working, and the willingness to end what isn’t.
