What this means for your business ↓

In practice

A possibility needs an owner.

You do not need a finished use case. You need a worthwhile outcome, someone accountable for the work, and a way to learn whether it is improving.

Own the outcome.

A business sponsor names the result, gives the work authority, and keeps it tied to a priority that matters.

Own the change.

An operating owner understands the workflow and the people using it. They help turn a promising idea into a usable practice.

Own what must hold.

A technology steward makes sure the data, systems, safeguards, and architecture can support the work.
