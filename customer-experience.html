The customer meets the experience. The workforce creates it.

AI earns its place when it helps a customer get to resolution and helps the workforce retain the context, decisions, and knowledge that make good service possible—not when it simply inserts automation between a person and an answer.

External — the customer

A handoff should not send the customer back to the beginning.

Recognition, intent, the current issue, and relevant history should travel with the interaction so the next conversation begins with understanding, not recovery.

Internal — the workforce

The workforce should not have to recreate what it already knows.

UC and collaboration platforms generate working intelligence in calls, meetings, chats, and decision threads. AI can capture it, make it searchable, and bring it back when people need to serve, sell, decide, or act.

Where AI earns its place

Improve the response. Retain what the business learns.

Start where service loses context: routing, live support, follow-through, and the patterns across customer and workforce conversations. The right capabilities should improve the work without adding another disconnected tool.

For the customer

Route the issue, not just the call.

Use intent, history, and urgency to guide the interaction to the right person, queue, or answer before the customer has to explain the issue again.

Find recurring failure sooner.

Review the full body of interactions to spot repeat contacts, emerging friction, and product feedback—not only the small sample quality teams can inspect.

Keep the story intact.

Make relevant history and intent available as the customer moves from chat to phone to email to a human.

For the workforce

Capture the work without extra work.

Turn calls, meetings, and working sessions into a searchable record with clear next actions, rather than leaving the follow-through to notes, inboxes, and memory.

Bring working knowledge back when it matters.

Make approved information, prior answers, relevant expertise, and the decisions that came before available where people already collaborate and serve.

Turn conversation into operating priorities.

Use customer patterns, meeting decisions, summaries, and workflow signals to show the responsible team what needs attention.

Make every interaction improve the one that follows.

AIR helps connect customer service and internal collaboration to the contact center, UC platforms, shared knowledge, automation, data, and the infrastructure beneath them.
