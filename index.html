The technology

The architecture underneath.

The technology behind the six dimensions: what gets designed, bought and run. Open any layer to see what it covers.

  • Network & connectivityA reliable path through the business.+

    Internet, private lines, fiber, mobile and SD-WAN carry information between locations, applications and clouds, with SASE bringing security into the same path. The right mix depends on where work runs and the bandwidth and resilience it needs. AI raises the bar on both.

    Internet & private linesEthernet & dark fiberMobile / LTESD-WANSASE / SSECloud networking
  • Data center & cloudThe right home for every workload.+

    Public cloud, colocation, private cloud and bare metal each suit different work. Monitoring, cost management and recovery keep capacity, service quality, spending and recoverability visible as demand grows, which matters more once AI workloads arrive.

    ColocationPrivate cloudBare metalDisaster recoveryStorage as a serviceCloud cost optimizationMigration
  • Data & integrationInformation that’s connected, organized and trusted.+

    Integration and data platforms bring information together from the systems it lives in, keep definitions consistent and permissions intact, and make answers traceable. This is the layer AI draws on, so look at how each option connects to the systems you already run and who keeps it current.

    Data movement & pipelinesData integrationData platformsAnalytics & decision intelligenceEnterprise searchData quality & governance
  • CybersecurityProtection across the environment.+

    Identity and access, endpoint protection, data loss prevention and around-the-clock detection and response, sized to the team you have. Evaluate how each option controls access, flags suspicious activity and supports a coordinated response, including what people paste into AI tools.

    vCISO & policyCompliance testingManaged firewallMDR / XDRSIEM & monitoringIdentity & access
  • Communication & collaborationHow your people talk and work together.+

    Calling inside Teams, Zoom or Webex, unified communications and mobility, and replacing copper lines before they fail. Meeting summaries and assistants now ship inside these platforms, so it’s worth knowing what your license already includes before paying for another tool.

    Teams, Zoom & Webex voiceUCaaSMobilityPOTS replacementSIP
  • Customer experienceWhere customers feel the difference.+

    Cloud contact center, self-service, workforce and quality tools, and outsourced support when you need more hands. This is often where AI earns its first return, so look at how each platform connects to your customer records and how results are measured.

    Contact center (CCaaS)Self-service & IVRWorkforce managementQuality & recordingOutsourcing (BPO)
  • Artificial intelligenceManaged access to AI, with rules people can use.+

    AI workspaces, model-access platforms, virtual agents and automation give people an environment for using AI. Look for connections to your business applications and controls over which models can be used. Every answer is only as good as the information behind it: relevant sources, preserved permissions and traceable answers. Policy and audit tools document what’s in use and under which rules; people still set policy and own decisions.

    AI workspaces & model accessVirtual agentsAgent assistConversational AIIntelligent automationAI governance & securityAI-ready data centersGPU as a service
  • Managed ITThe back office, handled.+

    Help desk, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace management, desktops, field services and backup keep tools working after launch, so questions don’t stall adoption and your team can spend its time on what moves the business.

    Microsoft 365 & Google WorkspaceHelp deskDesktop as a serviceField servicesBackupStaff augmentation
