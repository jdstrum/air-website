The AIR readiness framework

Readiness shows up in six places.

The tool is only one part of the decision. AI puts pressure on the data, systems, security, and people around it. The six dimensions show where the work needs attention.

Strategic Direction

How will the business change?

The workflows, decisions, and experiences that will evolve—and the outcome leadership intends to create.

Data Readiness

Can the right knowledge be reached?

Whether the information AI needs is trusted, connected, and available to power the output.

Technology Infrastructure

Can the environment carry it?

What the network, cloud, devices, systems, and integrations can support as workflows evolve and change.

Governance & Security

What is protected—and what is retained?

The perimeter, access, and guardrails protect the business. A secure gateway and knowledge layer retain the intelligence created.

Experience Layer

Does context survive the handoff?

Where employee, customer, contact-center, and collaboration experiences need to remain connected.

Workforce & Adoption

How will people work differently?

AI changes the work around people, removing some tasks and raising the value of judgment where it matters most.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

What makes Jai different from a generic AI chat?

Jai is trained on the AIR readiness framework. She is designed to listen for the conditions that make AI useful inside a business, not to offer generic AI advice.

Do I need to have an AI project already picked out?

No. Start with the decision, constraint, or opportunity that has your team thinking about AI. The conversation helps make the right next question clearer.

Why does AIR ask for a few details about my company?

When you choose to get a tailored readiness read or continue with a Resultant, those details help us understand your context and bring the right human into the conversation.

When does a person join the conversation?

Jai is the starting point. When the picture calls for judgment on priorities, architecture, or technology options, a Resultant continues the conversation with you.
