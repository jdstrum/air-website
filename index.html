THE SIX DIMENSIONS OF READINESS

Build a foundation
that holds.

Useful AI depends on more than the product you choose. These six dimensions reveal whether your organization has the direction, information, technology, safeguards, and human capability to support it.

Readiness extends beyond any one project. Improvements here can serve the business today and support future uses of AI. You can begin with a focused application while addressing the conditions it depends on.

Assess where your organization stands across all six dimensions.

THE ARCHITECTURE UNDERNEATH

What supports
the possibilities.

The six dimensions assess your organization’s readiness. These seven layers make the technology behind that readiness more tangible. Explore examples of product categories and the role each can play.

01PlatformManaged access to AI.+

AI workspaces, model-access platforms, and agent-building tools give people an environment for using AI. Look for connections to your business applications and controls over which models can be used.

Technology Infrastructure ↗
02GovernanceRules people can use.+

Data classification, policy management, and AI inventory tools help document what is being used and under which rules. Approval workflows and audit records support oversight; people still set policy and own decisions.

Governance & Security ↗
03IntelligenceInformation with meaning.+

Data integration, data quality, enterprise search, and knowledge management tools prepare and connect the information AI draws on. Their value depends on relevant sources, preserved permissions, and traceable answers.

Data Readiness ↗
04ConnectivityA reliable path through the business.+

Business connectivity, SD-WAN, and private cloud connections carry information between locations, applications, and computing environments. The mix depends on where work runs and the bandwidth and resilience it needs.

Technology Infrastructure ↗
05SecurityProtection across the environment.+

Identity and access management, endpoint protection, data loss prevention, and threat detection tools protect the environment. Evaluate how they control access, flag suspicious activity, and support a coordinated response.

Governance & Security ↗
06EnablementCapability people can put to work.+

Collaboration platforms, learning systems, and in-application guidance help people adopt new ways of working. They can put instruction and support closer to the task, alongside training and management support.

Workforce & Adoption ↗
07ScaleOperation that can sustain growth.+

Cloud infrastructure, performance monitoring, cloud cost management, and backup and recovery tools support wider use. They help teams track capacity, service quality, spending, and recoverability as demand grows.

Technology Infrastructure ↗

These are examples, not a shopping list. A product may serve more than one layer, and your existing tools may already cover several. Priorities depend on the work you want to support and the gaps that matter.
